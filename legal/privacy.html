Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Leap CRM ("Leap", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Leap CRM application (including any workspace hosted under leapcrm.tech) or our website at theleapcrm.com.

1. Information we collect

Leap is a CRM platform that your organization ("Customer") uses to manage its own contacts, deals, and communications. Most information in the product is entered or connected by Customer, on Customer's behalf, and Leap processes it to provide the service.

  • Account information: name, email address, and workspace membership for anyone who signs in to a Leap workspace.
  • CRM records: contacts, companies, opportunities, notes, tasks, and other records Customer or its users create or import.
  • Messaging and communications data: when a workspace connects a channel, Leap processes the content needed to display and send those communications — for example WhatsApp messages sent and received through the WhatsApp Business Platform, or synced email and calendar data from a connected account.
  • Billing information: if a workspace has a paid subscription, billing details are collected and processed by our payment processor, Stripe. Leap does not store full payment card numbers.
  • Usage and log data: standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, and access timestamps, used for security, troubleshooting, and keeping the service reliable.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the CRM service, including delivering and displaying messages sent through connected channels such as WhatsApp.
  • To authenticate users and secure workspaces.
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • To provide customer support and respond to inquiries.
  • To monitor, diagnose, and fix technical issues.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Leap does not sell personal information, and does not use Customer data to train third-party advertising products.

3. How information is shared

We share information with service providers who process it on our behalf, strictly to operate the service:

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of Leap, our customers, or the public.

4. Data retention

We retain information for as long as a workspace remains active, and for a reasonable period afterward as needed for legal, tax, or legitimate business purposes. Customer administrators can request export or deletion of their workspace's data by contacting us.

5. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect information, including encryption in transit (TLS) for all traffic, encryption of sensitive credentials at rest, and per-workspace data isolation. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal information we hold about you. If your organization's data is involved, we will generally direct your request to the relevant workspace administrator, since they control that data. To make a request, contact us at the address below.

7. International data transfers

Information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

8. Children's privacy

Leap is a business product and is not directed at, or knowingly used to collect information from, children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data can be sent to support@theleapcrm.com.
